# Fishing Temp Buoy

A floating, anchored data logger that records water temperature
throughout a fishing trip, so I can compare it against when I catch fish.

## Question
How does water temperature at my spot change over a day, and does it
line up with when I catch fish?

## v1 Requirements
- **Measures:** water temperature at the surface and ~1 m depth, plus air temperature inside the float
- **Sample rate:** every 5 minutes
- **Runtime:** at least 24 hours on one charge
- **Waterproofing:** floats at the surface; survives splashing, rain, and a brief dunk
- **Deployment:** tethered to a small anchor so it stays in place
- **Data:** logged to a microSD card, retrieved after each trip
- **Size / cost:** fits in a tackle bag, under $60 in parts
- **Environment:** freshwater (saltwater planned for v2)

## Success criteria
v1 works if it logs a full day on the water without leaking or losing data.
